Simple process

Three steps to better customer loyalty.

Issue rewards via our API, customers claim them in their Loot vault, and redeem across your partner ecosystem.

Step 1

Issue

Call Loot's API with reward details—type, value, expiry. We handle the rest: generation, storage, security, compliance.

Step 2

Claim

Customers get a link, QR code, or email. They click, create their Loot vault, and claim the reward instantly.

Step 3

Redeem

Rewards live in their vault and can be redeemed at your store, partner apps, or exchanged for other perks.
